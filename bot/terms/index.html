Terms of Service

For the Do Not Research Discord bot and the members portal it works with. In effect from 16 November 2026.

What this covers

These terms cover the Do Not Research Discord bot and the Discord features of the Do Not Research members portal: linking a Discord account to a membership, the roles the bot gives in the Do Not Research Discord server, and the counting of a linked member's posts and reactions for patches. They are run by Do Not Research Incorporated, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

By linking a Discord account, a person agrees to these terms and to the privacy policy. Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines also apply to everything done on Discord.

Who can use it

Anyone with a Do Not Research membership and a Discord account they are allowed to hold under Discord's rules, which require users to be at least 13 years old, or older where local law says so. A person may link one Discord account to one membership, and only an account that is their own.

What the bot does

When a Discord account is linked to an active membership, the bot gives that account the role of the membership (Member, Supporter, Sustainer or Facilitator) in the Do Not Research server. When the membership ends, is cancelled or is refunded, the bot takes the role back. Contributors whose link to their published work has been confirmed also get the Sleeper role. The bot only removes a role that it gave; roles given by a person are left alone.

For linked accounts, the bot counts posts, threads started and reactions in the Do Not Research server, including posts made before the account was linked, and the members portal uses those numbers to award patches. By linking a Discord account, a member agrees to this counting. The bot does not read or keep the text of messages, and keeps nothing about people who have not linked an account. What it keeps is described in the privacy policy.

Membership

Memberships are bought, changed and cancelled on ITM (itm.studio), under ITM's terms. The role follows the membership: it is a way into members' channels, not a purchase in itself, and has no value apart from the membership.

Patches

Patches are shown on a member's page in the portal for things they have done with Do Not Research, some of them on Discord. They are for fun and recognition: they have no cash value, are not a purchase, and cannot be transferred. The numbers behind them can be late or slightly off, for example when the bot was not running, when a post was in a place the bot cannot see, or when a post was deleted. Do Not Research may change what a patch is given for, and may remove a patch that was earned by spam, by reacting to one's own or coordinated posts, or by otherwise breaking the server's rules. If a number looks wrong, write to info@donotresearch.org.

Conduct

The role gives access to members' spaces in the Do Not Research Discord. Those spaces have their own rules, set by Do Not Research and posted in the server. Officers and moderators may remove a role or a person from the server for breaking those rules, whatever the state of the membership.

Do not try to obtain the role for an account that is not yours, to interfere with the bot or the portal, or to use them for anything other than joining the Do Not Research Discord as a member.

Availability

The bot and the portal are run by a small nonprofit and are provided as they are, without any guarantee that they will always be available or free of mistakes. A role may arrive or be removed late. If a role is wrong, write to info@donotresearch.org and a person will fix it.

Do Not Research may change, pause or stop the bot at any time. To the extent the law allows, Do Not Research is not liable for losses that come from using the bot or from it being unavailable.

Ending the link

A member can ask to unlink their Discord account or delete what is held about it at any time, as described in the privacy policy. Unlinking deletes the activity numbers, and the patches that depend on them go with them. Do Not Research may end a link if these terms are broken.

Changes

If these terms change, the new version is posted at this address with a new date. Continuing to keep a Discord account linked after a change means accepting the new terms.
