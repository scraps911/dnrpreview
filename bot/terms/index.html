What this covers

They are run by Do Not Research Incorporated, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines also apply to everything done on Discord.

By taking part in the Do Not Research Discord server, and by linking a Discord account on the members portal, a person accepts these terms and the Privacy Policy, which says what the bot reads and keeps.

Who can use it

Anyone in the Do Not Research server with a Discord account they are allowed to hold under Discord's rules. A person may link one Discord account to one membership, and only an account that is their own.

What the bot does

It gives and takes back roles that follow a membership, a class or published work, and only removes a role that it gave. It reads what is posted in the channels it can see, the list of people in the server, events and who attends sessions, and Do Not Research keeps and uses these as the Privacy Policy describes: for roles and patches, classes and events, its records and history, reports for its officers, and the server's rules. It may post in the server and may send a direct message about a membership, role, class or event.

Membership

Memberships are bought, changed and cancelled on ITM (itm.studio), under ITM's terms. A role follows the membership: it is a way into members' spaces, not a purchase in itself, and has no value apart from the membership.

Patches

Patches are shown on a member's page in the portal for things they have done with Do Not Research. They are for fun and recognition: they have no cash value, are not a purchase and cannot be transferred. The numbers behind them can be late or slightly off. Do Not Research may change what a patch is given for, and may remove a patch earned by spam or by breaking the server's rules. If a number looks wrong, write to info@donotresearch.org.

Conduct

The server has its own rules, set by Do Not Research and posted there. Officers and moderators may remove a role for breaking them, whatever the state of the membership. Do not try to obtain a role for an account that is not yours, or to interfere with the bot or the portal.

Availability

The bot and the portal are run by a small nonprofit and are provided as they are, without any guarantee that they will always be available or free of mistakes. A role may arrive or be removed late. If a role is wrong, write to info@donotresearch.org and a person will fix it. Do Not Research may change, pause or stop the bot at any time. To the extent the law allows, Do Not Research is not liable for losses that come from using the bot or from it being unavailable.

Ending the link

A member can ask to unlink their Discord account, or to have what is held about them deleted, at any time, as the Privacy Policy describes. Do Not Research may end a link if these terms are broken.
